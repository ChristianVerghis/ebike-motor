ebike-motor — what we're aiming for

Coreless dual-rotor axial flux rear hub. 240 mm OD, 12 coils / 14 poles per rotor, 1"×1"×3/8" N42 magnets, 10 mm stator, 2 mm air gaps, 48 V VESC FOC. ~350 W cruise, ~38 Nm launch at 80 A.
